# Reference research: top-selling photography print sites (2026-09-04)

Compiled for the demo theme overhaul. Hover behaviour unverified (text fetches). Portfolio-only sites (Dyar, Locardi, Wanger, Harvey, Burkard main, Chin main) excluded as commerce references.

## Landscape / large format — reference: lik.com
Sites: lik.com, aaronreedphotography.com, treyratcliff.com, mpiphoto.dk, ryandyar.com.

Home: one hero (room-mockup lifestyle shot of a single new release), short copy, one button "Secure Your Edition". Light header, logo top-left, nav The Work / The Galleries / The Artist / Shop / The Vault. Sans throughout, light neutral chrome; darkness comes from photos. Below: New Releases, Best Sellers, The Vault. Aaron Reed: same skeleton + stat bar ("Over 6 Million Dollars ... Sold since 2012") + promo banner. Ratcliff: minimal, no prices, "Contact for pricing", celebrity quotes.

Gallery: uniform tile grid; caption = title + "Limited Edition" + price / "Contact for Pricing" / "Sold Out"; filters collection, colour (18 swatches), format (Panoramic/Square/Standard), orientation.

Product (lik.com/products/twilight-whisper): framed preview then in-home mockup; dropdowns Size, then Frame Finish, then Liner; $4,100 to 9,400; "45 Artist Proof / 250 Limited Edition"; CTAs Add to Cart + "Not Sure? Contact a Sales Team Member"; "ships in 4-6 weeks". Reed: four media (acrylic float, ChromaLuxe metal, Dibond, museum frame), Limited Edition of 50, 21 business days, free US shipping.

Copy: single-hero-single-CTA home; medium then size then frame; edition line "N Artist Proof / N Limited Edition"; secondary "contact a sales member" CTA on four-figure items.

## Lifestyle / editorial — reference: graymalin.com
Sites: graymalin.com, gettyimagesgallery.com (Slim Aarons), galerieprints.com, 20x200.com.

Home: promo bar first (free shipping / free framing), video hero, nav SHOP / LIFESTYLE / ABOUT, shop split Most Popular / All / By Destination / By Series. Sans, white page, saturated imagery. Collections = image tiles + "SHOP NOW". Prices on cards. Galerie Prints: logo-centred header, headline "Collectable Photography, Prints and Posters", "From: £165", Trustpilot, 10% newsletter hook.

Gallery: cards with title, "from" price, destination/series tags, 6 images per product. Galerie Prints crops square for uniform 4-col grid.

Product (graymalin.com/products/the-glamour-girl): 6 images incl. room mockups (desktop + mobile crops); size row showing print AND framed dimensions (Small 11.5x17 to Collector 42x63); frame style (archival w/ 4-ply mat, shadow box, print only); frame colour SWATCHES White/Black/Natural/Blue + anti-glare; "FREE FRAMING & SHIPPING"; per-size edition limits (50/75/25/20), certificate on Large+.

Copy: frame colour as swatches; size row with framed outer dims; edition limit per size; promo bar + free framing as the offer.

## Fine art / abstract — reference: lumas.com
Sites: lumas.com, yellowkorner.com, store.magnumphotos.com, saatchiart.com, 20x200.com.

Home: two stacked promo banners w/ countdown, headline "Signed & limited edition art", nav Discover / Shop / Artists, shop by budget (Under $250 to $5,000+). White, bold sans. Card badges Bestseller / New / Last Prints, strikethrough pricing, live "Art editions just sold" feed. Magnum: time-boxed "Signature Drop", "One Week Only". 20x200: "Art for Everyone Starting at $35", press logos.

Gallery: card = badge, artist, title, price, "x sizes available". Yellowkorner: price-rise cue ("acquiring value on date"). Saatchi: artist+country, title, price, medium, dims, "Limited Edition of X", "Ready to hang", heavy filters.

Product (lumas.com/pictures/jean_marie_ghislain/morning_flight/): per-size status (Sold out / Bestseller), "Limited edition: 150, signed", "High Demand", "90% of customers order mounting or framing", QR AR wall preview, Make an Offer, 60-day returns. Yellowkorner: "7 formats from $140 to $7,300", room visualiser. 20x200: fixed size/price ladder 8x10 $40 to 30x40 $1,950.

Copy: per-size sold-out state; "just sold" feed; time-boxed drops; fixed size/price ladder across the catalogue.

## Travel — reference: shop.jimmychin.com (weak field)
Sites: shop.jimmychin.com, stevemccurry.com, chrisburkardshop.com, treyratcliff.com.

Home: nav Shop Prints / Books / About, red accent on white, print rows by collection ("Free Solo", "Everest Limited Edition", "Tetons"), caption "Title | Location, Year" + Shop Now. McCurry: galleries by theme (Afghanistan, Monsoons, Portraits), white. Burkard: prints/books/merch/workshops.

Product: single image, five sizes 8x12 to 24x36, one paper, $275, framing on request, "Notify me". McCurry: no cart, signed/numbered, email to order.

Copy: collection-row home with "Title | Place, Year" captions. Travel shooters sell workshops/books first; borrow the landscape configurator, keep the travel caption format.

## Shared patterns + evidence
- ALL top sellers: white/neutral chrome, sans type. None use a dark UI (Lik's dark gallery wall is physical, not lik.com).
- Every serious seller shows a room mockup on the product page, most on the home hero; Lumas/Yellowkorner/Saatchi add a visualiser.
- Grids: uniform tiles, 3-4 cols, price on card. Galleries without price are portfolio sites.
- Edition language is always a number: total ("Limited Edition of 50") or per size.
- Saatchi: 70% of buyers hesitate without seeing work in place; +17% avg spend after "View in a Room" AR (8thwall.com case study). Aeolidia client: ~1pt conversion lift swapping lead photo for room mockup. Shopify photographer theme roundups converge on large editorial layouts, minimal chrome, badge/countdown support (Align, Highlight, California).
